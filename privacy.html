Gridshot Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

Gridshot does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your photos are processed on your iPhone and are never uploaded anywhere.

What Gridshot does with your photos

When you photograph a table or choose an image, Gridshot reads the text in it using Apple's text recognition, which is built into iOS and runs entirely on your device. The image and the resulting spreadsheet stay on your iPhone. Nothing is transmitted off the device at any point.

Gridshot works with no network connection at all, including in airplane mode.

Information Gridshot does not collect

  • No name, email address or account of any kind
  • No photos, images or extracted table contents
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • No usage analytics, crash telemetry or behavioural tracking

Gridshot contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about you is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Permissions Gridshot asks for

  • Camera — used only while you are taking a photo of a table. Gridshot cannot access the camera at any other time.
  • Photos — when you pick an image, iOS passes that single image to Gridshot. Gridshot has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Information stored on your device

Gridshot saves a small amount of information locally so the app works as expected, such as how many exports you have made and whether you have purchased the unlock. This stays on your iPhone, is not readable by anyone else, and is deleted if you delete the app. Exported CSV and spreadsheet files are saved wherever you choose to put them.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; Gridshot never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction, and Gridshot only receives confirmation from iOS that the purchase is valid.

Children

Gridshot is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Data protection laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Gridshot holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to request, export or erase. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gridshot ever changes how information is handled, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy or about the app: gridshot.support@gmail.com
